# 1.5.4 Page Improvements and Checklist Verification

This round retained the existing quotation, pricing, owner-approval and activity-record workflows. It completed the two items concerning long inquiries and test waiting conditions from the teammate's “1.5.3 One-Time Correction Checklist”, along with small page-layout improvements. No database tables or external paid APIs were added.

## Page Changes

- Kept the blue-and-white palette while adjusting text contrast, card borders and button hierarchy. Reduced top whitespace on narrow screens so the current step appears sooner.
- Completed steps show a check mark; the current step remains blue. Approved and passed states use green, while pending states use amber.
- The product page shows whether review is in progress, passed or awaiting confirmation, with a visible progress indicator while waiting. The backend response remains authoritative.
- Ordinary quantity evidence is collapsed with the original text to reduce repeated numbers. Invalid, ambiguous and correction-required content remains visible. Users can expand the original text and quantity evidence.
- Original-price and recommended-price cards have a clearer hierarchy and aligned digits. The owner's decision and final outputs still use the existing backend calculations.
- The inquiry area adds three-language input guidance, a live character count and a notice that each batch allows at most 40 product lines. Error notices can be dismissed by button or keyboard without clearing the original inquiry.
- Layouts were checked in Chinese, English and Malay at 320, 430 and 1160px. Features still appear step by step: customer requirements, product review, quote confirmation and customer reply.

## Two Checklist Fixes

| Checklist item | Implementation | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 product segments triggered HTTP 500 and RecursionError | backend/requirements.py now uses an explicit stack, caches product positions and separately limits product count and processing work. An over-budget CatalogError consistently returns 422, without producing a quotable result for any part of the inquiry | 27 new boundary tests and 1 formal long-input retest; parse took 0.1126 seconds and review 0.1113 seconds, health returned 200 for both, and quote/review counts remained 0 |
| copilot_browser checked the preview amount too early | The original formal script now waits for cpPreview to contain 495.00, with a 10-second timeout. All stock, amount, approval, export and feedback assertions remain. Failures save a screenshot, the current preview and backend logs | The corrected tests/copilot_browser.mjs was run directly; all seven groups passed |

Splitting is limited to 40 product requirements, 160 original nonempty segments, 160 pending ranges, 320 stack pops and 512,000 cumulatively scanned characters. Delivery, greetings and similar text follow the existing classification rules and do not directly consume the product allowance, but extreme amounts of non-product text are still constrained by the processing budget. Unknown products cannot bypass the product limit merely by failing to match the catalog. The system does not truncate to the first 40 products and continue quoting, and does not raise the recursion limit.

Valid 40-line inquiries were tested with six separator forms. Original spans, left-to-right order, quantities and source_id remained intact; delivery continuation lines and greetings did not consume the product allowance. Forty lines with missing quantities still prompt for clarification under the existing rules. Forty-one lines or any budget overrun reject the whole inquiry. Inputs near 12,000 characters remain budget-controlled; requests above the character limit are still rejected by request-body validation.

Evidence of the original HTTP 500 and the two preview failures was preserved. The previous local 1.5.3 pass did not establish that the old test waiting condition was reliable. This round verified the corrected formal script again.
