# Complete workflow coverage

Based on the team's “AI Quote Copilot complete project workflow,” application version 1.6.1.

This release follows the team's existing decision to use a no-API approach. Requirement rules, stock matching, historical statistics, owner approval, and feedback all run locally. Local rules are not presented as a real LLM, and historical correlations are not described as causal effects of discounts.

| Original workflow stage | Corresponding feature in this release |
| --- | --- |
| 01 Customer inquiry | WhatsApp Web message entry, selected text, and manual web input |
| 02 Understand requirements | Common Chinese, English, and Malay expressions; produces category, quantity, unit, application, specification, preference, area, and brand; retains complete numbers and separate rows for different products with equal quantities; handles common negatives and conditions on subsequent lines; asks for missing quantity/delivery area or conflicting details |
| 03 Match internal data | Products + Inventory; a brand is optional; recommends in-stock products matching intended use/preferences, showing Best Match, Alternatives, specification, stock, unit price, and reasons |
| 04 Original price | Server-side Decimal unit price × quantity and base delivery rules; separately displays original product amount, base delivery fee, and original total |
| 05 Pricing Intelligence | Discounts from similar won orders, conversion rate among closed orders, recent won sales, inventory thresholds, actual local delivery costs, maximum discount, and minimum estimated gross margin |
| 06 Owner approval | Displays original and recommended prices together; accepts the recommendation or approves after editing discount, delivery fee, and notes; staff cannot approve, and the server validates the rules |
| 07 Formal quotation | Quotation number, customer, line items, original product prices, discount, delivery fee, final total, approver, and date; PDF and customer replies in three languages |
| 08 Retain business data | Saves the original recommendation, owner changes and approval snapshot, customer outcome, and actual delivery cost; new quotations use the updated history without rewriting old revisions |

Verified example: 20 bags × RM23 gives an original product amount of RM460. The recommended 3% discount and RM25 delivery fee produce a recommended total of RM471.20. After the owner changes these to 4% and RM30, the approved PDF, Excel, and replies in all three languages use RM471.60.

The initial sample has 8 similar closed orders, 5 of them won. Recording another won order with a 4% discount changes the sample to 9 orders and 6 wins, with an average won-order discount of 3.17%. This is a persisted data update, not a fixed screenshot or prerecorded response.

## Page interaction

Staff use the home page and step-by-step quotation workflow; owners use approval cards. Alternative products, recognition details, historical evidence, price adjustments, and return-for-revision controls are collapsed by default. Review prompts expand when requirements differ, and manual changes record specific values and reasons. Settings centralize imports and optional integrations. Quotations can be filtered by pending approval, follow-up, and closed status.

This round further reduces empty space at the top of narrow screens, emphasizes original and recommended prices, shows review progress and input counts, and allows notices to be dismissed. Evidence for ordinary quantities is collapsed with the original inquiry; unusual quantities and manual corrections remain prominent. Each request supports up to 40 product requirements. Exceeding the product limit or parsing-work budget returns a batch-submission prompt for the entire request without selecting only some products.

## Current implementation boundaries

- Requirement parsing uses local rules and supports the examples and common building-material expressions. Arbitrary natural language, unrecognized uses, and mixed descriptions of several products may require manual correction. Separate lines are recommended for multiple products. Conditions with unclear ownership, double negatives, and conflicts block progress; this is not general natural-language understanding.
- Historical recommendations use explainable statistics and rules. No paid large model is trained or called, and statistically optimal pricing is not promised.
- Inventory comes from an Excel snapshot; stock receipt/issue, reservation, and order fulfillment are not implemented.
- Customer outcomes must be recorded by a person. Owner approval is not treated as a won sale.
- Customer replies are placed in a draft and sent by a person; PDFs are attached separately. Real automatic mobile notifications are not enabled.
- One store, fixed owner and staff accounts, and local data; no cross-computer synchronization or production public deployment.
- No integration test has been performed with a real WhatsApp account, real external APIs, a native Windows extension, or a public tunnel. See VERIFICATION.md for the scope of synthetic DOM and local HTTP checks.

The optional mobile approval page also supports viewing and editing recommendations and approving with a password, and has been tested through a real local gateway. No messages were sent to real phone numbers.

## 1.6.1 colors and layout

The brand is consistently EzQuote: dark green sidebars and buttons, white cards, a light gray-green background, and mint green recommended amounts. Wide screens use a sidebar and split overview/record panels; narrow screens use compact top navigation. Approved, pending, and returned states have different colors. Only existing menus and actual quotation statistics are shown; fictional amounts, conversion rates, and unimplemented features from the reference image are not used. Customer/product summaries and price recommendations can be viewed side by side on desktop, while mobile retains the existing review steps.
