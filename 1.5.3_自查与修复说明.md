# 1.5.3 Self-Review and Fix Notes

Historical record: this document describes the checks performed for 1.5.3 at the time. A teammate's later retest found an error on long inquiries and a waiting-condition risk in the complete business browser script. Both were corrected in 1.5.4. See VERIFICATION.md and 1.5.4_优化与验收说明.md for the current results.

This round revisited the earlier issues involving truncated numbers, omitted products, negative conditions, backend corrections and upgrade safety. Alongside the existing regressions, new edge inputs and deliberately delayed browser responses exposed four further types of problems. They were fixed while retaining no-API mode, the existing blue-and-white interface and the quotation workflow.

## New Findings and Fixes

| Issue | Behavior reproduced in 1.5.2 | Handling in 1.5.3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Number words lacked grammar validation | twenty and thirty became 50; two two became 4; one million twenty ignored the first part and used 20; 二十二十 became 40 | Validates the complete supported Chinese, English or Malay number grammar first. Invalid or unsupported expressions retain their original text and require clarification. Valid expressions such as two hundred and twenty and dua ratus dua puluh still work |
| Product-omission checks still depended on some conjunctions | cement & sand, plus sand, 与/及, a new sentence or special line breaks could leave only the cement | Checks matched product text for unexplained content. Recognizing one product alone is not enough to permit quoting. Unexplained content prompts the user to split lines or rewrite the inquiry, and the backend refuses to issue a review ID. CR and Unicode line breaks also preserve requirement segments |
| Curly-apostrophe negation was not recognized | I don’t want fast setting was interpreted as a request for fast-setting cement | Supports common straight- and curly-apostrophe negation. Suffixes not yet fully classified, such as unwanted and 免了, prompt for clarification instead of silently recommending the opposite product |
| Late desktop responses overwrote current work | Clicking A and then B allowed A's late response to switch the page back to A. Saving, approval and similar actions had related asynchronous risks | Isolates responses using a workspace sequence number and quote ID/revision. Authorized saves, submissions or approvals on the original quote complete normally without overwriting a new draft or another quote. Source confirmation is rechecked before saving; PDF filenames and activity records remain bound to the original quote |

The original 1.5.2 release package was extracted and tested separately, without changing its source. All three semantic problems were also reproduced through creation, submission and approval. The desktop quote-switching issue was reproduced with delayed responses in a real browser. Results are in qa-results/audit-v152-before-after.json, audit-browser-before.txt and upgrade-v152-v153.json. All tests used synthetic customers and isolated data.

## Backend Enforcement

Numbers still preserve the complete expression and original positions before validation. The new number_words.py is shared by local quantity scanning and numeric-evidence validation for the optional model. It prevents arbitrary addition of number words or ignoring unknown words and calculating only the suffix. Invalid number-word expressions return quantity_format.

The new uncovered_text blocking issue identifies content that still needs verification after explicit products, quantities, specifications, conditions, logistics and supported request wording have been accounted for. Even directly submitting a specific SKU and quantity, or forging a “confirmed” correction value, cannot obtain a valid review ID. The inquiry must be clarified, split into lines or rewritten and then parsed again. This check conservatively requests clarification for some valid but unsupported expressions; it is not general natural-language understanding.

Clearly separated unknown products can still be completed, or removed after customer cancellation with a specific recorded reason. Normal manual quantity, SKU and delivery changes still require confirmed values and reasons. No reason can override insufficient stock.

## Desktop Page Verification

The new audit_browser.mjs contains eight groups of checks:

1. A delayed response opening quote A cannot overwrite quote B selected afterwards.
2. When approval of the original quote completes, the customer and original text in a new draft remain intact.
3. Submission following a save still targets the original request, without clearing a new draft or its cache.
4. Changes made to the same draft while a save request is pending remain intact and can be reviewed and submitted as the next revision.
5. Changing the customer while waiting for a backend review invalidates source confirmation and prevents a silent save.
6. Delayed polling for a completed quote cannot replace another pending quote currently being viewed.
7. A PDF download after switching quotes retains the original filename; the preparation record is written only to the original quote.
8. Invalid Chinese, English and Malay number words and unexplained products are blocked. Three-language messages and the 320px page remain readable, and editing the inquiry allows the workflow to continue.

## Upgrade Data Verification

Three approved quotes and one pending quote were created in the original 1.5.2 program. Version 1.5.3 then opened the same data directory directly, with the following verified results:

- Original accounts, password hashes and sessions were preserved; database initialization remained repeatable.
- The amounts and PDF bytes of all three approved quotes remained unchanged.
- The old pending quote indicated that another review was required. Its old approval request returned 409. After the original inquiry was corrected and a newly reviewed second revision was saved, it could be submitted and approved again.
- Old v4 browser drafts retained the customer and original inquiry, but required parsing again before continuing.

Approved history is not automatically recalculated during an upgrade, so previously generated incorrect documents are not silently overwritten either. To correct one, create a revision from the original quote, review and approve it again, and retain the original approval history.
