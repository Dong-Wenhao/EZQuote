## Delivery Scope of This Round

All 187 backend tests, 47 groups across seven browser scripts and 10 extension-logic groups passed. The standard complete sample recommendation remained RM471.20, and the owner-adjusted sample remained RM471.60. Developer logs and structured results from each script were included in the package; browser screenshots and generated demonstration data were excluded from the installer.

Verification used local services on Linux, a simulated Chrome extension bridge and simulated WhatsApp DOM. Real WhatsApp accounts, first-time Windows installation, the native Chrome side panel and real mobile notifications still needed local integration testing; none were reported as “passed”. No-API mode was retained, with no paid models called or real messages sent.
